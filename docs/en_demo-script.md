# RobotOps Copilot Demo Script

## 1. 3 – 5 expositions for the item

### Part I: Background and issues (approximately 40 seconds)

- The diagnosis of robotic failure relies on logs, engineering manuals, status data and live photographs.
- Direct use of LLM has problems with non-source completion, loss of authority and high-risk recommendations.
- The objective of the project is to read diagnostic aids only, not automatically.

### Part II: Structure (approximately 90 seconds)

- Document ingestion: Parsing, Chunk, Embedding and Chroma.
- Retrieving: Normalization, dual-way keywords and vectors, RRF, rearrangement and evidence gate.
- Agent: Planner is responsible for the next selection, Runner Control Cycle, Excelctor Validation Tool.
- Security: the former Planner safety classification and minimum range of tools at request level.
- Multimodal: Photo verification, request level reference, Vision structured observation and source layer.
- Delivery: SSE, session, console, export and Docker.

### Part III: Key trade-offs (approximately 60 seconds)

- Tool privileges are not given to the model.
- The reference metadata are not generated by the model.
- Vision observation does not amount to a work fact.
- Offline Fixture and real model results are tabulated.
- High-risk requests can be observed but must be manually reviewed.

### Part IV: Results and limitations (approximately 60 seconds)

- 30/30 Fixed offline scenes, 34/34 Security special and 2655/2655 automated tests.
- The true model was rigorous, 4/10, and human review of 6 passed, 2 part, 2 failed.
- Current constraints include real model stability, page-specific retrieval, uncalculated costs and no production infrastructure.

### Part V: Follow-up direction (approximately 30 seconds)

- Submission - Evidence overlay matrix.
- More stable structured Planner output.
- More search reordering and visual quality pre-checking.
- Complete certification, auditing, simulation and industrial safety reviews before real equipment is accessed.
